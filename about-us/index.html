Simplifying Your K-Electric Bill Experience

Who We Are

KE Duplicate Bill is an independent educational portal and blog created by energy analysts to help the citizens of Karachi understand their electricity tariffs and consumption. We do not provide software solutions or billing services. Our mission is to educate consumers on NEPRA's slab system, explain how to read meters, and provide free JavaScript estimation calculators so you can verify your official bills.

Our Mission

Our goal is to empower consumers with information. Beyond just bill checking, we provide comprehensive guides on:

  • Understanding Unit Prices and Tariff Slabs
  • Load Shedding Schedules
  • New Connection Procedures
  • Tax Certificates and Refunds
