Fact-Checking Policy

Our commitment to accuracy, data integrity, and editorial independence.

At KE Bill Guide, we understand that electricity tariffs, fuel price adjustments, and taxation policies are complex and financially impactful for the citizens of Karachi. As an independent educational portal, our primary responsibility is to provide information that is unequivocally accurate, verified, and up-to-date.

1. Primary Sourcing

We do not rely on unverified news outlets for tariff data. All rates, slabs, and billing logic implemented in our calculators and guides are sourced directly from primary regulatory documents:

  • NEPRA (National Electric Power Regulatory Authority): Official tariff notifications and S.R.O.s (Statutory Regulatory Orders) published in the Gazette of Pakistan.
  • Ministry of Energy (Power Division): Official policy announcements regarding subsidies and Protected Consumer status.
  • FBR (Federal Board of Revenue): Guidelines on Advance Income Tax and GST applicable to utility bills.

2. Data Verification Process

Before any tariff change or billing guide is published or updated on our platform, it undergoes a verification process:

  • Cross-Referencing: Our energy analysts cross-reference the proposed data against the latest NEPRA S.R.O.
  • Mathematical Verification: The logic in our custom JavaScript calculators (such as the Bill Estimator) is tested against real, redacted K-Electric bills to ensure the calculated output matches official tariff rules.
  • Editorial Review: Content is reviewed before publication.

3. Corrections Policy

If an error is identified in our guides or calculator math, we correct the error immediately upon verification. We encourage our users to report discrepancies by contacting us directly through our Contact Us page.

4. Independence and Objectivity

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or funded by K-Electric or any government body. Our guides and tools are created objectively from a consumer perspective.
