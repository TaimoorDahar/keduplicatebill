Fact-Checking Policy

Our commitment to accuracy, data integrity, and editorial independence.

At KE Duplicate Bill, we understand that electricity tariffs, fuel price adjustments, and taxation policies are complex and financially impactful for the citizens of Karachi. As an independent educational portal, our primary responsibility is to provide information that is unequivocally accurate, verified, and up-to-date.

1. Primary Sourcing

We do not rely on third-party blogs or unverified news outlets for tariff data. All rates, slabs, and billing logic implemented in our calculators and guides are sourced directly from primary regulatory documents:

  • NEPRA (National Electric Power Regulatory Authority): Official tariff notifications and S.R.O.s (Statutory Regulatory Orders) published in the Gazette of Pakistan.
  • Ministry of Energy (Power Division): Official policy announcements regarding subsidies and Protected Consumer status.
  • FBR (Federal Board of Revenue): Guidelines on Advance Income Tax and GST applicable to utility bills.

2. Data Verification Process

Before any tariff change or billing guide is published or updated on our platform, it undergoes a strict verification process:

  • Cross-Referencing: Our energy analysts cross-reference the proposed data against the latest NEPRA S.R.O.
  • Mathematical Verification: The logic in our custom JavaScript calculators (such as the Bill Estimator) is manually tested against real, redacted K-Electric bills to ensure the calculated Output matches the official ledger exactly (accounting for rounding differences).
  • Editorial Approval: All content must be signed off by a senior editor before being marked as "Fact Checked".

3. Corrections Policy

Despite our rigorous processes, errors can occur—especially when government tax policies change mid-billing cycle. If an error is identified:

  • We correct the error immediately upon verification.
  • For significant errors affecting our offline calculators, we append an "Update Log" at the bottom of the tool noting what was changed and when.
  • We encourage our users to report discrepancies by contacting us directly through our Contact Us page.

4. Independence and Objectivity

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or funded by K-Electric or any government body. Our guides, troubleshooting steps, and tutorials are written objectively. When we explain how to file a complaint or claim a refund, we do so strictly from the perspective of consumer rights.
