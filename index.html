Understanding Your K-Electric Billing Cycle

Managing electricity costs in Karachi requires a solid understanding of how K-Electric structures its billing. At KE Duplicate Bill, our mission is to provide you with the tools and educational resources necessary to decode your consumption and take control of your expenses.

The Slab System

Pakistan uses a progressive tariff structure. The rate per unit increases dramatically as you cross thresholds. Staying below the 200-unit mark ensures you remain in the "Protected" consumer category.

Fixed Charges & Taxes

Every bill includes 1.5% Electricity Duty, 18% General Sales Tax (GST), and Advance Income Tax (which is exempted if you are an active tax filer).

Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA)

FPA reflects the fluctuating cost of fuel used to generate electricity globally. It is usually applied to your bill 2 months after the actual consumption month.

How to Download Your KE Duplicate Bill Online

Lost your paper bill or need another copy for documentation? K-Electric makes it easy to retrieve a duplicate bill anytime. You can utilize the embedded portal above, or use mobile services like WhatsApp and SMS.

Get Your KE Bill on WhatsApp

K-Electric's automated WhatsApp chatbot at 0348-0000118 is an excellent alternative if you have a slow internet connection. It provides instant PDF downloads directly in your chat app.

Check Your KE Bill via SMS

If you lack internet access entirely, the SMS service provides your bill amount and due date. Send messages to 8119 from any registered mobile network. To Register: Send REG [13-digit A/C#] to 8119. To Check Bill: Send BILL [13-digit A/C#] to 8119.
