How to Check & Download Your Official K-Electric Bill

To view your official duplicate bill statement directly from K-Electric:

  1. Locate your 13-digit Account Number on a previous physical bill (located top-right).
  2. Visit K-Electric’s Official Web Portal.
  3. Enter your 13-digit account number into the official bill lookup field.
  4. View or download the official PDF statement for payment or tax filing.
