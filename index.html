What a duplicate bill is—and what this site does

A duplicate bill is another copy of a bill already issued by K-Electric, Karachi’s electricity supplier. It is useful when the printed copy is lost or a payment provider asks for bill details. This independent guide explains the process; it does not issue bills, handle payments, or have access to customer accounts.

Find your reference

Keep a previous K-Electric bill nearby. The bill portal asks for the 13-digit account number shown with your customer details. Do not post that number publicly or send it to an unrelated person.

Use the official record

The portal can display the bill K-Electric has issued. Its amount, due date, and payment status are the record to rely on; estimates and screenshots are not a substitute.

Check before you pay

Confirm the account number, billing month, payable amount, and due date on the official copy. Payment channels and eligibility can change, so check K-Electric’s current instructions.

Before you open the bill portal

  1. Locate a previous bill and read the 13-digit account number from the customer-information area.
  2. Have your mobile phone available if K-Electric asks for verification.
  3. Use a private connection and enter details only on K-Electric’s official portal.
  4. Save or print the displayed bill only after checking the account and billing month.

How to Download Your KE Duplicate Bill Online

If the portal does not load, open K-Electric’s official website directly and look for its current bill-service option. Browser extensions, a weak connection, or a mistyped account number can all prevent a result.

Get Your KE Bill on WhatsApp

K-Electric publishes its customer-contact channels and may update them. Use the current contact details on its official website rather than a forwarded message. Never share OTPs, card details, or account credentials in a social-media chat.

Check Your KE Bill via SMS

For payment, choose a channel currently listed by K-Electric or your bank/app and keep the receipt until the payment appears on the account. If a payment is missing, contact the payment provider and K-Electric with the transaction reference—this website cannot trace it.

Common questions

I do not have an older bill. What can I do?

Use K-Electric’s official customer support route and be ready to verify the connection details. Do not guess account numbers or use another person’s bill.

The portal says my account number is invalid.

Re-enter all 13 digits without spaces. Confirm that you are using the account number, not a meter number or a payment reference. If it still fails, ask K-Electric for account-specific help.

Can this site accept a bill payment?

No. This site is informational only. Payments should be made through a channel approved by K-Electric or your financial institution.

Why does the amount differ from my estimate?

An issued bill can include the billing period, tariff category, taxes, adjustments, arrears, and credits. The issued bill and K-Electric’s explanation are authoritative.
