Understanding Your K-Electric Duplicate Bill

An independent reference guide explaining how to locate account credentials on your paper statement, decode billing charges, and access official electronic bills.

Independent Resource Notice: KE Bill Guide does not issue, store, or modify utility bills. Official bills must be obtained directly via K-Electric’s Official Website.

1. Account Number (13-Digit) vs. Consumer Number (8-Digit)

Every K-Electric statement contains two distinct identification numbers located at the top of your physical bill:

  • 13-Digit Account Number: Starts with 04000.... This is the primary credential required for online bill lookup, bank payments, and mobile wallet transactions.
  • 8-Digit Consumer Number: Identifies your physical meter hardware connection for technical service requests and field inquiries.

2. Breakdown of K-Electric Bill Components & Taxes

Component Description Calculation Basis
Variable Energy Cost Base charges for electricity consumed based on slab rates. Units consumed × Applicable NEPRA Slab Rate.
Fuel Charges Adjustment (FCA) Monthly adjustment reflecting actual generation fuel costs. Set monthly by NEPRA per unit credit or surcharge.
Electricity Duty (ED) Provincial tax assessed on energy charges. 1.5% of Variable Energy Cost.
General Sales Tax (GST) Federal sales tax on total utility billing. 18% assessed on cumulative bill subtotal.
Income Tax (Section 235) Advance tax withholding applied to non-filer domestic bills over Rs. 25,000. 7.5% withholding tax rate (Adjustable in tax filing).

3. Official Retrieval & Payment Guidance

To check or pay your current bill statement safely:

  1. Retrieve your 13-digit account number from a past paper statement.
  2. Visit K-Electric’s official website (`ke.com.pk`) or open your mobile banking app.
  3. Verify that the consumer name and address match before completing online payment.
  4. Retain digital transaction IDs for billing records.
