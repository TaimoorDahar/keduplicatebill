K-Electric Load Shedding: How Schedules Work & Where to Check

An independent guide explaining K-Electric's Aggregate Technical & Commercial (AT&C) loss-based load management policy in Karachi and how to access official area schedules.

Official Schedule Notice: Real-time feeder shutdown schedules are managed exclusively by K-Electric. To check current outages for your specific neighborhood, visit K-Electric’s Official Web Portal or SMS 8118.

1. How K-Electric Categorizes Load Shedding

Under NEPRA regulations, K-Electric calculates load management hours based on the Aggregate Technical & Commercial (AT&C) loss percentage of each feeder circuit serving a neighborhood. Feeders with lower power theft and higher bill payment recovery receive fewer outage hours.

Feeder Loss Category Loss Percentage Range Daily Outage Estimate Typical Area Profile
Exempt (Low Loss) Below 20% AT&C Loss 0 Hours (Exempt) Industrial zones, strategic facilities, high-recovery residential areas.
Low to Medium Loss 20% to 40% AT&C Loss 2 to 4 Hours Moderate recovery residential neighborhoods.
High Loss 40% to 60% AT&C Loss 6 to 7.5 Hours Areas with moderate losses and billing arrears.
Very High Loss Above 60% AT&C Loss 8 to 10+ Hours High theft zones with significant commercial recovery deficits.

2. How to Check Official Outage Schedules for Your Area

  • KE Live Mobile App: Log in with your 13-digit Account Number to view live feeder status for your location.
  • SMS Notification (8118): Register your mobile number by texting REG <Space> 13-Digit Account No to 8118 to receive SMS alerts prior to scheduled maintenance shutdowns.
  • Official Web Portal: Visit K-Electric’s official website and enter your consumer details under the load shedding inquiry section.
