K-Electric New Connection Application Guide

Independent guide outlining required documentation, connection fees, and application procedures for residential and commercial electricity meters in Karachi.

Official Application Notice: KE Bill Guide is an independent educational portal. Applications must be submitted through K-Electric's official e-Services portal at new-connections.ke.com.pk.

1. Required Documents Checklist

Before initiating an application, ensure you have scanned copies of the following verified documents:

  • Applicant Identity: Valid CNIC of the property owner/applicant.
  • Proof of Ownership: Sale deed, lease deed, allotment letter, or registered power of attorney.
  • Wiring Test Report: Electrical wiring fitness certificate issued by a licensed wiring contractor.
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC): Required if the property is rented or has multiple joint owners.
  • Neighbor Bill Copy: Copy of a recent K-Electric bill from an adjacent premises to verify feeder location.

2. Residential vs Commercial Connection Categories

Category Load Limit Meter Type Description
Residential (Single-Phase) Up to 5 kW Single-Phase Static Meter Standard domestic homes, apartments, small residential units.
Residential (Three-Phase) Above 5 kW to 15 kW 3-Phase Whole-Current Meter Large homes with multiple AC units or high power equipment.
Commercial (C1 / C2) Up to 40 kW (C1) / Above 40 kW (C2) Commercial 3-Phase Meter Shops, offices, commercial plazas, and business establishments.

3. Application Process Steps

  1. Submit Online Form: Visit KE's official new connection portal and fill in applicant details.
  2. Site Inspection: K-Electric field survey team visits the location to verify load feasibility and transformer capacity.
  3. Demand Notice Issuance: KE issues a Demand Notice for connection security deposit and capital cost estimate.
  4. Fee Payment: Deposit the Demand Notice amount at designated bank branches.
  5. Meter Installation: K-Electric technical staff installs the meter and energizes the supply.
