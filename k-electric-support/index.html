K-Electric Customer Support & Complaint Guide

Independent guide to reaching K-Electric customer service, registering billing or outage complaints, and escalating unresolved disputes to NEPRA.

Official Service Disclaimer: KE Bill Guide is an independent informational portal. To log complaints directly with K-Electric, use their official helpline 118 or visit K-Electric’s Official Website.

1. Official K-Electric Support Channels

K-Electric operates multiple 24/7 customer care channels for Karachi residents:

Channel Contact / Access Best Used For
24/7 Call Center Call 118 or (021) 99000 Urgent power outages, electrical hazards, emergency complaints
WhatsApp Support +92 348 0000118 Duplicate bill link request, tax certificate, complaint status tracking
SMS Service 8118 (Register via REG <Space> Account No) Load shedding updates, quick outage reports
KE Live App Google Play Store / Apple App Store Bill history view, online complaint logging, outage tracking
Customer Care Centers Physical Centers across Karachi zones Meter inspection requests, ownership change, billing disputes

2. How to Register a Complaint with K-Electric

  1. Identify the issue type: Categorize whether your issue is an emergency outage, wrong meter reading, overbilling, or technical fault.
  2. Have your 13-digit Account Number ready: Located on the top-right corner of any previous K-Electric paper bill.
  3. Log through Call Center or App: Call 118 or log a ticket via the official KE Live mobile application.
  4. Save your Complaint Reference Number: KE assigns a unique ticket ID. Always note this down for follow-up tracking.

3. Escalating Unresolved Billing Disputes to NEPRA

If K-Electric fails to resolve a billing dispute or meter complaint within statutory timelines, consumers in Pakistan have the right to escalate to NEPRA (National Electric Power Regulatory Authority):

  • File a written complaint with the NEPRA Regional Consumer Affairs Division in Karachi.
  • Provide copies of your disputed bill, proof of payment history, and your KE complaint ticket reference number.
  • NEPRA Consumer Affairs can order meter testing or bill revisions under the Consumer Service Manual guidelines.
